## Full description — English

Find Your People. Find Your Place.

Student Flatmate Finder helps college students in India find compatible flatmates and affordable rooms near campus — without brokers and without sharing your phone number with strangers.

HOW IT WORKS
• Build your profile in four quick steps: budget, college, move-in date and lifestyle.
• See a compatibility score with clear reasons — budget, timing, tidiness, sleep schedule, smoking, food habits and more.
• Like or pass. When you both like each other, it's a match.
• Chat privately inside the app. Your number and email stay hidden.

ROOMS & VACANT BEDS
• Browse flats, shared rooms and vacant beds near your college.
• Clear rent and deposit details, amenities and photos.
• Have a vacant bed? List it free. The exact address is shared only with students whose enquiry you accept.

TRUST & SAFETY
• Badges show exactly what was checked: Email Verified, College Email Verified, Student ID Reviewed and Listing Reviewed.
• Badges reduce risk but don't guarantee anyone's identity — our Safety centre explains what each one means.
• Report and block on every profile, listing and message, reviewed by real moderators.
• Built-in reminders: always view the property and get a written agreement before paying.

YOUR PRIVACY
• Gender and age stay hidden unless you choose to show them.
• Pause discovery, download your data or delete your account at any time.

Free for students. No brokerage. Operated by NoidaRankers.
