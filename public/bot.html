NoidaRankersBot

NoidaRankersBot is the low-volume crawler of the NoidaRankers backlink platform. It fetches individual public pages to (a) evaluate whether a page is a relevant, editorial resource and (b) verify whether a link that a site owner agreed to publish is present.

  • It obeys robots.txt (user-agent token NoidaRankersBot) including Crawl-delay.
  • It makes at most one request every few seconds per site, never logs in, never submits forms and never executes JavaScript.
  • To opt out, add User-agent: NoidaRankersBot / Disallow: / to your robots.txt.
